# What Iranian konkour students pay, and how many of them there are

Researched 2026-09-14 (۲۳ شهریور ۱۴۰۵). All prices in toman, observed that day. Sources are linked
per figure; the gaps are listed at the end and one of them is large.

## The market is not small, and it is not segmented

**~520,000–650,000 arshad candidates a year, and every one of them sits the same زبان عمومی
paper.** 521,420 participants in 1404 ([jamaran](https://www.jamaran.news/fa/amp/news-1659821));
651,250 registrations for 1405 across two windows, against 146,637 admission seats
([mehrnews](https://www.mehrnews.com/news/6937455/)). Earlier years ran higher still — 694,417 in
1399, 950,429 in 1393.

This closes the per-field question the owner raised (R11). The addressable market is *every arshad
candidate*, not the candidates of one field code. Field is a presentation filter, never a market.

Representative book range **320,000–800,000, typically ~450,000–550,000 after discount**. A
candidate buying the standard two- or three-book vocabulary set spends **1–1.6 million toman on
print alone**, and a single 20-hour English class costs more than the exam fee.

## Who pays on Cafe Bazaar

From Bazaar's own 1402 annual report ([zoomit summary](https://www.zoomit.ir/tech-iran/424276-bazaar-annual-report-1402/)):
**5.5 million users bought digital content against 55.8 million registered accounts — about 10%
pay for anything in a year.** 2,000 میلیارد تومان was paid out to developers across 46 million
transactions, which works out to roughly **43,000 toman of developer revenue per transaction**
(our arithmetic, not theirs; the average is dominated by games and small purchases). The 1403
report gives 50 million monthly actives but publishes no monetisation figures — the paying-user
series appears to stop at 1402.

## The competitive shelf

| Segment | Installs |
|---|---|
| Top Iranian English apps (فست دیکشنری, لرنیت, زبانشناس) | 1–1.7 million |
| Mid-tier (دهکده زبان, همیارزبان) | 300k–600k |
| Vocabulary / flashcard niche (پاکِتلی, وردیلا, لینگوگیم) | 77k–97k |
| **Konkour-vocabulary apps specifically** | **all under 20k, and abandoned** |

Every konkour-labelled vocabulary app found was last shipped in 1398–1399 or has been delisted by
its developer. One paid-up-front app (لایتنر هوشمند, 10,000 toman) has 3.2k installs and last
shipped in 1399. Note also [وردیلا](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/app.lingoacademy.words) — 83k
installs, updated مرداد ۱۴۰۵, and its description explicitly targets لغات پرتکرار کنکور ارشد و
دکتری. It is the closest thing to a live competitor.

**This is an empty shelf, and the evidence does not say why.** Either nobody has served this niche
properly, or nobody has managed to monetise it. Both readings fit the data equally well, and
distinguishing them is what the first selling season is for.

## Pricing practice

- **Free install plus in-app purchase is universal.** Paid-up-front apps are rare, tiny and dead.
- **Nobody publishes their price.** Neither Bazaar nor Myket shows the IAP ladder on the web, and
  زبانشناس's public pricing page
  ([zabanshenas.com](https://zabanshenas.com/zabanshenas-app-price/)) deliberately refuses to name
  a number: «برای اطلاع از قیمت … اپلیکیشن را نصب کنید». Withholding the price *is* the practice.
- **Discounting is the default state, not a campaign.** Six of seven book pages opened that day
  carried an active discount (8%, 9%, 10%, 40%, 65%); همیارزبان was running a 50% "بازگشت به مدرسه"
  banner with a countdown. A page with no discount looks broken.
- **The free tier is gated by consumables, not by time.** همیارزبان gives free "keys" daily;
  زبانشناس sells a 10,000-toman consumable beside its tiers. Unlocking content per unit is more
  common than a trial that expires.
- **Both permanent licences and subscriptions exist** and students recognise both. لرنیت has
  historically sold an «اشتراک ویژه بدون محدودیت زمانی» alongside 3/6/12/24-month plans.
- Bazaar supports subscriptions and **dynamic, targetable discounts (تخفیف پویا)** natively, and
  charges 400,000 rials (40,000 toman) a year to publish.

## The largest gap

**No current in-app price for any Iranian language app could be verified.** Neither store publishes
the ladder, and the only concrete numbers found are from 2019 — seven years and one currency
collapse out of date. Closing this needs an Android device and half an hour of installing
competitors. Until then, every price comparison here is anchored on **books and courses**, which
are published, rather than on apps, which are not.

Other unverified items: مدرسان شریف's own store would not load, so their book price comes from a
reseller; Digikala is JS-rendered and returned nothing; the 1405 exam date is inconsistent across
sources; and 651,250 is registrations, not exam-day attendance.
